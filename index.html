Recognize school violence

School violence can take many forms.

School violence is not only physical. It can affect students through actions, words, relationships, unwanted sexual behavior, or online spaces.

Physical Violence

Hitting, kicking, pushing, fighting, or intentionally causing physical injury.

Psychological Violence

Threatening, humiliating, isolating, spreading rumors, or causing emotional distress.

Sexual Violence

Unwanted sexual behavior that violates someone’s boundaries, dignity, or safety.

Bullying

Repeated harmful or aggressive behavior, especially when there is an imbalance of power.

Cyberbullying

Using messages, images, videos, social media, or online platforms to threaten, humiliate, or harm someone.
