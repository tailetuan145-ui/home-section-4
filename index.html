Recognize school violence

School violence can take many forms.

School violence is not only physical. It can affect students through actions, words, relationships, unwanted sexual behavior, or online spaces.

Physical Violence

Hitting, kicking, pushing, or causing physical harm.

Psychological Violence

Threatening, humiliating, isolating, or causing emotional harm.

Sexual Violence

Unwanted sexual behavior that violates another person's boundaries.

Bullying

Repeated harmful behavior involving an imbalance of power.

Cyberbullying

Using online spaces to threaten, shame, harass, or harm someone.
